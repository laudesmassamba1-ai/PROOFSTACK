# Visual QA guidelines

Purpose:

- Validate design is consistent across landing and app, and the new particle identity aligns with PROOFCORE brand.

Process:

- Take screenshots of key pages at 1280x800 and mobile widths.
- Check palette usage, typography scales, spacing, and component styling.
- Validate dark/light theme transitions.
- Validate particle animation performance and fallback behavior.

Checklist:

- Logo formation animation appears correctly in hero on all target resolutions.
- Cards and rails have consistent radii and shadows.
- Buttons have correct hover/focus states and contrast.
- Typography scales are consistent with UI_REDESIGN.md.
- No fake data or mock interactions in production components.
